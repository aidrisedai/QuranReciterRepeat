COMPREHENSIVE TAJWEED RULES - REACH THE GOAL VIA TAJWEED RULES
==================================================================
Compiled by Maha Rashed (1st Edition)

This document contains authoritative Tajweed rules based on classical Islamic scholarship.
Use ONLY these rules when providing feedback on Quran recitation.

SOURCE: "Reach the Goal Via Tajweed Rules" - Teacher's Class Notes
Based on: Al-Jazariyyah, Tuhfat al-Atfal, Hidayat al-Qari, and other classical sources

1. MAKHARIJ AL-HURUF (ARTICULATION POINTS OF LETTERS)
================================================================================

1.1 AL-JAWF (The Empty Space - Throat/Mouth Cavity)
   Letters: ا (Alif), و (Waw with Dhammah), ي (Ya with Kasrah)
   - These are the Madd letters (elongation letters)
   - Pronounced from the empty space in throat/mouth

1.2 AL-HALQ (The Throat) - 6 Letters
   Upper Throat: ء (Hamzah), ه (Ha)
   Middle Throat: ع ('Ayn), ح (Ha)
   Lower Throat: غ (Ghayn), خ (Kha)

1.3 AL-LISAN (The Tongue) - 18 Letters
   
   a) Aqsa al-Lisan (Base of Tongue):
      - ق (Qaf): From furthest back part of tongue touching soft palate
      - ك (Kaf): Slightly forward from Qaf
   
   b) Wasat al-Lisan (Middle of Tongue):
      - ج (Jeem), ش (Sheen), ي (Ya): Middle of tongue against hard palate
   
   c) Tarful-Lisan (Tip of Tongue):
      - ض (Dhad): Edge of tongue against upper molars (UNIQUE to Arabic)
      - ل (Lam): Tip of tongue against gum line of upper front teeth
      - ن (Noon): Tip just below Lam position
      - ر (Ra): Tip vibrating slightly above Noon position
   
   d) Tathal-Lisan (Tip touching upper teeth):
      - ط (Ta), د (Dal), ت (Ta): Tip against upper front teeth
   
   e) Between tip and teeth edge:
      - ص (Sad), ز (Zay), س (Seen): Tip near but not touching teeth
   
   f) Tip between upper and lower teeth:
      - ظ (Dha), ذ (Thal), ث (Tha): Tongue tip visible between teeth

1.4 ASH-SHAFATAIN (The Two Lips) - 4 Letters
   - و (Waw): Both lips rounded
   - ف (Fa): Lower lip touching upper teeth
   - ب (Ba), م (Meem): Both lips pressed together

2. SIFAT AL-HURUF (CHARACTERISTICS OF LETTERS)
================================================================================

2.1 SIFAT LAZIMAH (Essential Characteristics with Opposites) - 5 Pairs

   a) AL-HAMS vs AL-JAHR (Whispering vs Loud)
      Hams (10 letters): ف ح ث ه ش خ ص س ك ت
      Mnemonic: "فَحَثَّهُ شَخْصٌ سَكَتَ"
      Jahr (rest of letters): Voiced/loud pronunciation

   b) ASH-SHIDDAH vs AR-RAKHAWAH (Hardness vs Softness)
      Shiddah (8 letters): أ ج د ق ط ب ك ت
      Mnemonic: "أَجِدُ قَطٍ بَكَتْ"
      Rakhawah (rest): Soft, flowing pronunciation
      BAYNIYYAH (Between): ل ن ع م ر - neither hard nor soft

   c) AL-ISTI'LAA vs AL-ISTIFAL (Elevation vs Lowering)
      Isti'laa (7 letters - Heavy): خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ
      Mnemonic: "خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ"
      Istifal (rest): Tongue lowered, lighter sound

   d) AL-ITBAQ vs AL-INFITAH (Enclosing vs Opening)
      Itbaq (4 letters): ص ض ط ظ
      Tongue pressed toward roof of mouth, creates echoing
      Infitah (rest): Tongue released

   e) AL-IDHLAAQ vs AL-ISMAT (Fluidity vs Retention)
      Idhlaaq (6 letters): ف ر م ن ل ب
      Mnemonic: "فَرَّ مِنْ لُبِّ"
      Flow easily off tongue
      Ismat (rest): Require more effort

2.2 SIFAT LAZIMAH (Without Opposites) - 7 Characteristics

   a) AS-SAFIR (Whistling): ص ز س
   b) AL-QALQALAH (Echoing/Bouncing): ق ط ب ج د
      Mnemonic: "قُطْبُ جَدٍ"
      Greater Qalqalah: ط ق
      Lesser Qalqalah: ب ج د
   c) AL-LEEN (Softness): و ي when preceded by Fathah and followed by Sukoon
   d) AL-INHIRAF (Deviation): ل ر
   e) AT-TAKRIR (Repetition): ر only
   f) AT-TAFASHI (Spreading): ش only
   g) AL-ISTITAALAH (Elongation): ض only

2.3 TAFKHIM vs TARQIQ (Heavy vs Light Pronunciation)

ALWAYS HEAVY (Tafkhim):
   - Letters of Isti'laa: خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ
   - ل in لله (Allah) only when preceded by Fathah or Dhammah

CONDITIONAL (Ra ر):
   Heavy when:
      - Has Fathah: رَبِّ
      - Has Dhammah: رُسُل
      - Has Sukoon after Fathah: مَرْضَاةِ
      - Has Sukoon after Dhammah: غُرْفَة
   Light when:
      - Has Kasrah: رِجَال
      - Has Sukoon after Kasrah: مِرْيَة
      - Has Sukoon after broken Hamzah: أَأَنْذَرْتَهُمْ

3. AHKAM AN-NOON AS-SAAKINAH WAL-TANWEEN
   (Rules of Noon with Sukoon and Tanween)
================================================================================

3.1 AL-IDHHAR (Clear Pronunciation)
   When followed by throat letters: ء ه ع ح غ خ
   Example: مِنْ هَادٍ (clear noon with Haa)

3.2 AL-IDGHAAM (Merging)
   Letters: ي ر م ل و ن
   Mnemonic: "يَرْمَلُونَ"
   
   a) Idghaam with Ghunnah (nasal sound): ي ن م و
      Example: مَنْ يَقُولُ (noon merges with Ya with nasal)
   
   b) Idghaam without Ghunnah: ل ر
      Example: مِنْ رَبِّهِمْ (noon merges with Ra, no nasal)

3.3 AL-IQLAAB (Conversion)
   When followed by: ب (Ba)
   Noon/Tanween converted to hidden Meem with Ghunnah
   Example: أَنْبِئْهُمْ (noon becomes like Meem)

3.4 AL-IKHFAA (Hiding)
   Remaining 15 letters: ص ذ ث ك ج ش ق س د ط ز ف ت ض ظ
   Noon/Tanween hidden with nasal sound
   Example: مِنْ شَيْءٍ (noon hidden with nasal)

================================================================================
4. AHKAM AL-MEEM AS-SAAKINAH (Rules of Meem with Sukoon)
================================================================================

4.1 IDHHAR SHAFAWI (Labial Clarity)
   Before all letters EXCEPT م and ب
   Example: هُمْ فِيهَا (clear Meem)

4.2 IDGHAAM SHAFAWI (Labial Merging)
   When followed by another م (Meem)
   Example: كَمْ مِنْ (first Meem merges into second with Ghunnah)

4.3 IKHFAA SHAFAWI (Labial Hiding)
   When followed by ب (Ba)
   Example: تَرْمِيهِمْ بِحِجَارَةٍ (Meem hidden with Ghunnah)

================================================================================
5. AHKAM AL-MADD (Rules of Prolongation)
================================================================================

Madd Letters: ا (preceded by Fathah), و (preceded by Dhammah), ي (preceded by Kasrah)

5.1 MADD TABEE'I (Natural Prolongation)
   Duration: 2 counts (1 Alif)
   Example: قَالَ (Alif after Qaf with Fathah)

5.2 MADD WAAJIB MUTTASIL (Obligatory Connected)
   When Hamzah follows Madd in same word
   Duration: 4-5 counts
   Example: السَّمَاء (Alif before Hamzah in same word)

5.3 MADD JA'IZ MUNFASIL (Permissible Separated)
   When Hamzah follows Madd in next word
   Duration: 2, 4, or 5 counts (most common: 4)
   Example: بِمَا أُنْزِلَ (Alif in first word, Hamzah in second)

5.4 MADD LAZIM (Necessary Prolongation)
   When Madd followed by doubled letter (Mushaddad) or Sukoon
   Duration: 6 counts
   
   a) Madd Lazim Kalimi (In word): الصَّاخَّة
   b) Madd Lazim Harfi (In letters): الم (start of Surah)

5.5 MADD 'ARIDH LIS-SUKOON (Presented Stopping)
   When stopping on a word that has Madd
   Duration: 2, 4, or 6 counts
   Example: الْعَالَمِينَ (if stopping)

5.6 MADD LEEN
   و or ي with Sukoon preceded by Fathah
   Duration: 2, 4, or 6 counts when stopping
   Example: بَيْت (if stopping on it)

5.7 MADD SILAH (Connecting Prolongation)
   Haa of pronoun (هُ، هِ) between two moving letters
   
   a) Silah Sughra: 2 counts (not followed by Hamzah)
      Example: لَهُ ما
   
   b) Silah Kubra: 4-5 counts (followed by Hamzah)
      Example: مَالَهُ أَخْلَدَهُ

6. AHKAM AL-WAQF WAL-IBTIDA' (Stopping and Starting Rules)
================================================================================

6.1 TYPES OF WAQF (Stopping)

   a) Waqf Lazim (Necessary): م
      MUST stop here, meaning incomplete if continuing
   
   b) Waqf Mamnoo' (Prohibited): لا
      MUST NOT stop here, damages meaning
   
   c) Waqf Ja'iz (Permissible): ج
      May stop, but continuing is better
   
   d) Waqf Mujawwaz (Allowed): ز
      May stop or continue equally
   
   e) Waqf Murakhkhas (Conceded): صلى
      Better not to stop, but allowed due to breath
   
   f) Waqf Mutlaq (Unrestricted): 
      May stop anywhere breath runs out

6.2 RULES WHEN STOPPING

   a) Tanween becomes Sukoon (drop the -n sound)
      رَحِيمًا → رَحِيمْ
   
   b) Ta Marbuta (ة) becomes Ha with Sukoon
      رَحْمَةً → رَحْمَهْ
   
   c) Silent letters stay silent
      رَأَوْا → رَأَوْ (Alif silent)

6.3 IBTIDA' (Starting Rules)

   - Never start with Sukoon letter
   - Never start in middle of Idghaam
   - Never start on pronoun separated from reference
   - Start should complete meaning

================================================================================
7. AHKAM AL-LAM (Rules of Lam)
================================================================================

7.1 LAM ASH-SHAMSIYYAH (Solar Lam)
   Silent Lam before solar letters: ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن
   Example: الشَّمْس (Lam not pronounced, Sheen has Shaddah)

7.2 LAM AL-QAMARIYYAH (Lunar Lam)
   Clear Lam before lunar letters: ا ب ج ح خ ع غ ف ق ك م ه و ي
   Example: الْقَمَر (Lam pronounced clearly)

7.3 LAM LAFDHUL-JALALAH (Lam in word Allah)
   Heavy (Tafkhim): After Fathah or Dhammah
      Example: قَالَ اللَّهُ (heavy Lam)
   Light (Tarqiq): After Kasrah
      Example: بِسْمِ اللَّهِ (light Lam)

8. AHKAM AR-RA (Rules of Ra)
================================================================================

See Section 2.3 for comprehensive Ra rules.

Additional context:
- The letter after Ra may influence it
- If Ra has Sukoon, look at letter BEFORE it
- In case of doubt, Tafkhim (heavy) is default

9. COMMON MISTAKES (LAHN AL-KHAFI - Hidden Errors)
================================================================================

9.1 MAKHARIJ ERRORS
   - Mixing ض with ظ
   - Making ق sound like ك
   - Not distinguishing ح from ه
   - Weak pronunciation of ع and غ

9.2 SIFAT ERRORS
   - Not maintaining Qalqalah at stopping points
   - Light pronunciation of heavy letters
   - Heavy pronunciation of light letters
   - Insufficient Ghunnah duration

9.3 MADD ERRORS
   - Shortening Madd Muttasil
   - Over-extending Madd Tabee'i
   - Incorrect Madd Lazim duration

9.4 NOON/MEEM ERRORS
   - Clear pronunciation when should be hidden
   - Wrong Ghunnah duration
   - Forgetting Iqlaab

9.5 WAQF ERRORS
   - Stopping at prohibited places
   - Not dropping Tanween
   - Continuing where must stop

10. DETAILED CHARACTERISTICS WITHOUT OPPOSITES
================================================================================

10.1 AL-QALQALAH (The Echoing/Bouncing)
   Definition: A vibration or echoing sound at the end of pronunciation
   Letters: ق ط ب ج د (Mnemonic: قُطْبُ جَدٍ)
   
   THREE LEVELS OF STRENGTH:
   a) STRONGEST (Qalqalah Kubra): When stopping on Mushaddad Qalqalah letter
      Example: الْحَقّ (when stopping)
   
   b) STRONG (Qalqalah Kubra): When stopping on Sakin Qalqalah letter
      Example: صَدْرَكَ (when stopping on Qaf)
   
   c) WEAK (Qalqalah Sughra): When Sakin Qalqalah letter in middle of word
      Example: يَقْطَعُ (Qaf in middle)
   
   NOTE: Qalqalah is ONLY pronounced when letter is Sakin (has Sukoon or
         assigned Sukoon due to stopping)

10.2 AS-SAFIR (The Whistling)
   Definition: Natural whistle-like sound emitted while pronouncing
   Letters: س ز ص
   Example: صِرَاطَ، زَيْنٌ، سَبِيلِ

10.3 AL-LEEN (The Softness)
   Definition: Articulation with natural ease and softness
   Conditions:
   - Waw Sakin (و) with Fathah on letter before it
   - Ya Sakin (ي) with Fathah on letter before it
   Examples: خَوْف، بَيْت
   When stopping: Can be prolonged 2, 4, or 6 counts

10.4 AL-INHIRAF (The Deviation)
   Definition: Slight deviation of tongue toward another makhraj
   Letters: ل ر
   - Lam: Tongue deviates slightly toward Ra's makhraj
   - Ra: Tongue deviates slightly toward Lam's makhraj

10.5 AT-TAKRIR (The Repetition)
   Definition: Trilling of tongue causing letter to repeat
   Letter: ر ONLY
   IMPORTANT: Unlike other qualities, we must ABSTAIN from this quality
              Do NOT trill the Ra excessively - pronounce once

10.6 AT-TAFASHI (The Spreading)
   Definition: Spreading of sound in mouth while pronouncing
   Letter: ش ONLY
   Example: شَهِدَ

10.7 AL-ISTITAALAH (The Prolongation)
   Definition: Prolongation of sound throughout makhraj (1.5-1.75 beats)
   Letter: ض ONLY
   Applies when: Letter has Sukoon or Shaddah
   Example: أَرْضٍ

11. RULES OF NOON AND MEEM MUSHADDAD
================================================================================

11.1 NOON MUSHADDAD (نّ)
   Pronunciation: Make Ghunnah (nasal sound) for 2 beats
   Example: إِنَّ، ثُمَّ، عَمَّ
   NOTE: If reader stops on word ending with Noon Mushaddad, the Ghunnah
         must still be applied

11.2 MEEM MUSHADDAD (مّ)
   Pronunciation: Make Ghunnah (nasal sound) for 2 beats
   Example: أَمَّا، لَمَّا، ثُمَّ
   NOTE: If reader stops on word ending with Meem Mushaddad, the Ghunnah
         must still be applied

12. HEAVY AND LIGHT LETTERS (DETAILED)
================================================================================

12.1 ALWAYS HEAVY LETTERS
   The seven Isti'laa letters: خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ
   Mnemonic: "خُصَّ ضَغْطٍ قِظْ"
   Pronunciation: Back of tongue rises toward roof, creating heavy sound

12.2 ALWAYS LIGHT LETTERS
   All letters except: Heavy letters, Lam in Allah, Ra, Alif Madd
   Pronunciation: Tongue lowered (Istifal quality)

12.3 TEMPORARY HEAVY/LIGHT: LAM IN ALLAH
   a) HEAVY (Tafkhim):
      - When preceded by Fathah: قَالَ اللَّهُ
      - When preceded by Dhammah: يَشَاءُ اللَّهُ
      - When starting with Allah: اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
   
   b) LIGHT (Tarqiq):
      - When preceded by Kasrah: بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

12.4 TEMPORARY HEAVY/LIGHT: RA (ر)
   See detailed Ra rules in Section 8
   
12.5 TEMPORARY HEAVY/LIGHT: ALIF MADD (ا)
   - If letter BEFORE Alif Madd is heavy → Alif Madd is heavy
     Example: صِرَاطَ (Sad is heavy, so Alif is heavy)
   - If letter BEFORE Alif Madd is light → Alif Madd is light
     Example: قَالَ (Qaf is heavy, so first Alif is heavy)

12.6 TEMPORARY HEAVY/LIGHT: GHUNNAH FOR IKHFAA HAQIQI
   - If following letter is heavy → Ghunnah is heavy
     Example: مِنْ قَبْلُ (Qaf is heavy, so Ghunnah is heavy)
   - If following letter is light → Ghunnah is light
     Example: مِنْ شَيْءٍ (Sheen is light, so Ghunnah is light)

13. COMPREHENSIVE MADD (PROLONGATION) RULES
================================================================================

13.1 MADD TABEE'I (Natural Madd)
   Duration: 2 beats (1 Alif)
   Condition: Madd letter not followed by Hamzah or Sukoon
   Examples: مَالِكِ، تُوبُوا، كِتَابٌ

13.2 MADD AL-BADAL (Substitute Madd)
   Duration: 2 beats
   Condition: Every Hamzah preceding a Madd letter
   Examples: آمَنَ، أُوتُوا، إِيمَانًا
   If NOT followed by Sakin/Hamzah: 2 beats only

13.3 MADD 'EWADH (Compensation Madd)
   Duration: 2 beats
   Condition: Replacing Tanween Fathah with Alif Madd when stopping
   Examples: سَبِيلًا → سَبِيلَا، رَحِيمًا → رَحِيمَا
   EXCEPTION: Taa Marbuta (ة) - pronounce as Haa with Sukoon, not with Madd

13.4 MADD 'ARIDH LIS-SUKOON (Presented for Stopping)
   Duration: 2, 4, or 6 beats (reader's choice, commonly 4)
   Condition: Madd Tabee'i followed by letter that becomes Sakin due to stopping
   Examples: الرَّحِيمِ (when stopping), نَسْتَعِينُ (when stopping)
   NOTE: This Madd ONLY exists if reader stops. If continuing, it's Madd Tabee'i (2 beats)

13.5 MADD AL-LEEN (Easy/Soft Madd)
   Duration: 2, 4, or 6 beats (when stopping)
   Condition: Waw/Ya Sakin preceded by Fathah, at end of word when stopping
   Examples: خَوْفٌ، البَيْتِ، الصَّيْفِ
   If reader doesn't stop: No Madd applied

13.6 MADD WAAJIB MUTTASIL (Obligatory Connected Madd)
   Duration: 4-5 beats (commonly 5)
   Condition: Madd letter followed by Hamzah in SAME word
   Examples: جَاءَ، السَّمَاءِ، سُوءُ، جِيءَ

13.7 MADD JA'IZ MUNFASIL (Permissible Separated Madd)
   Duration: 2, 4, or 5 beats (commonly 4-5)
   Condition: Madd letter at end of word, Hamzah at beginning of NEXT word
   Examples: بِمَا أُنزِلَ، فِي أَنفُسِهِمْ، قَالُوا آمَنَّا
   NOTE: If stopping on first word, becomes Madd Tabee'i (2 beats)

13.8 MADD LAZIM (Necessary Madd) - 6 BEATS ALWAYS
   Four Types:
   
   a) MADD LAZIM KALIMI MUTHAQQAL (Word, Emphasized)
      Condition: Madd followed by Mushaddad letter in same word
      Duration: 6 beats
      Examples: الضَّالِّينَ، الطَّامَّةُ، الصَّاخَّةُ
   
   b) MADD LAZIM KALIMI MUKHAFFAF (Word, Lightened)
      Condition: Madd followed by Sakin letter in same word
      Duration: 6 beats
      Example: آلْآنَ (ONLY occurrence in Quran)
   
   c) MADD LAZIM HARFI MUTHAQQAL (Letter, Emphasized)
      Condition: Madd in letter followed by Mushaddad
      Duration: 6 beats
      Examples: الم (Lam-Meem merged), المص (Lam-Meem merged)
      Letters: ن ق ص ع س ل ك م
   
   d) MADD LAZIM HARFI MUKHAFFAF (Letter, Lightened)
      Condition: Madd in letter followed by Sakin
      Duration: 6 beats
      Examples: ن، ص، ق (at beginning of Surahs)
      Letters with Madd: ك ه ي ع ح ط س م
   
   NOTE: Letters at beginning of Surahs like ر ط ه have only 2 beats (Madd Tabee'i)

14. STOPPING AT END OF WORDS (WAQF RULES)
================================================================================

14.1 WORDS ENDING WITH TANWEEN
   Change Tanween Fathah to:
   - Fat-hah on last letter + Alif Madd
   Example: رَحِيمًا → رَحِيمَا

14.2 WORDS ENDING WITH TAA MARBUTA (ة)
   Change to: Haa (هـ) with Sukoon
   Example: رَحْمَةٌ → رَحْمَهْ
   NO MATTER which Harakah is on the Taa Marbuta

14.3 WORDS ENDING WITH EXTRA MADD SIGN
   Ignore the extra Madd sign at end, make normal Madd (2 beats)
   Examples: قَالُوا (ignore final Alif when stopping)

14.4 WORDS ENDING WITH ANY HARAKAH (Other than Tanween Fathah)
   Change to: Sukoon on last letter
   Examples: نَسْتَعِينُ → نَسْتَعِينْ

14.5 WORDS ENDING WITH SAKIN/MADD LETTERS
   Stop with Sukoon, no change
   Examples: قَدْ، مَعَ، وَلَدَ اللَّهُ

14.6 WORDS ENDING WITH MUSHADDAD LETTER
   Two rules:
   a) If Qalqalah letter: Stop with strong Qalqalah and small voice jerk (Nabr)
      Example: الْحَقّ
   
   b) If Meem or Noon Mushaddad: Stop with Ghunnah for 2 beats
      Example: ثُمَّ
   
   c) Any other letter: Stop with no Harakah and make Ghunnah
      Example: رَبِّ

16. IMPORTANCE AND BENEFITS OF LEARNING TAJWEED
================================================================================

16.1 RELIGIOUS OBLIGATION
Muhammad bin Al-Jazaree (9th Century Hijri) states in his famous poem:
"And applying Tajweed is an issue of absolute necessity,
whoever doesn't apply Tajweed to the Qur'an, then a sinner is he."

The majority of scholars agree that applying Tajweed rules is an individual
obligation (فرض عين) upon every Muslim who recites the Qur'an.

16.2 PROPHETIC EXAMPLES
- Umm Salamah described the Prophet's (SAW) recitation as "clearly distinguished
  letter by letter" [Tirmidhi]
- Sa'eed bin Mansoor relates that Abdullah bin Mas'ood corrected a man's recitation,
  specifically on elongation: "Lil Fuqaraaaa-i wal masaakeen" [Sunan]

16.3 BENEFITS OF LEARNING TAJWEED
a) "The reciters of the Qur'an will be in the company of the noble and obedient angels"
   - Hadith: "Verily the one who recites the Qur'an beautifully, smoothly, and precisely,
     he will be in the company of the noble and obedient angels. And as for the one who
     recites with difficulty, stammering or stumbling through its verses, then he will
     have TWICE that reward." [Al-Bukhari and Muslim]

b) "You will be from the best of people"
   - Hadith: "The best of you are the ones who learn the Qur'an and teach it to others"
     [Al-Bukhari]

c) "There are ten rewards for each letter you recite from the Quran"
   - Hadith: "Whoever reads a letter from the Book of Allah, he will have a reward.
     And that reward will be multiplied by ten. I am not saying that 'Alif, Laam, Meem'
     is a letter, rather I am saying that 'Alif' is a letter, 'laam' is a letter and
     'meem' is a letter." [Tirmidhi - Sahih]

d) "The Qur'an will lead you to Paradise!"
   - Hadith: "The Qur'an is an intercessor, something given permission to intercede,
     and it is rightfully believed in. Whoever puts it in front of him, it will lead
     him to Paradise; whoever puts it behind him, it will steer him to the Hellfire."
     [At-Tabaraanee - Authentic]

16.4 TWO TYPES OF MISTAKES
Scholars divide mistakes into:

a) CLEAR MISTAKES (اللحن الجلي)
   - Must be avoided by all
   - Changes meaning or pronunciation obviously
   - Considered a sin if done deliberately when able to correct
   - Example: Saying ض as ظ, or changing Fathah to Kasrah

b) HIDDEN MISTAKES (اللحن الخفي)
   - Lighter ruling
   - Recitation is lacking in completeness but acceptable
   - Example: Insufficient Ghunnah duration, weak Qalqalah

16.5 RECITING MELODIOUSLY
The Prophet (SAW) commanded: "Beautify the Qur'an with your voices [for a fine
voice increases the Qur'an in beauty]" [Bukhari]

"He who does not recite the Qur'an in a pleasant tone is not of us." [Abu Dawud]

"Truly the one who has one of the finest voices among the people for reciting
the Qur'an is the one whom you think fears Allah when you hear him recite."
[Daarimi, Tabaraani]

17. COMMON TAJWEED ERRORS TO AVOID
================================================================================

17.1 MAKHARIJ (ARTICULATION POINT) ERRORS
- Mixing ض (Dhad) with ظ (Dha) - ض is from edge of tongue, ظ from tongue tip
- Making ق (Qaf) sound like ك (Kaf) - Qaf is deeper in throat
- Not distinguishing ح (Ha) from ه (Ha) - ح is from middle throat, ه from upper
- Weak pronunciation of ع ('Ayn) and غ (Ghayn)
- Not touching teeth for ث، ذ، ظ letters

17.2 SIFAT (CHARACTERISTICS) ERRORS
- Not maintaining Qalqalah at stopping points (especially ق، ط)
- Light pronunciation of heavy letters (خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ)
- Heavy pronunciation of light letters
- Insufficient Ghunnah duration (must be 2 beats)
- Over-trilling the ر (Ra) - should not repeat excessively

17.3 MADD (PROLONGATION) ERRORS
- Shortening Madd Muttasil (should be 4-5 beats, not 2)
- Over-extending Madd Tabee'i (should be exactly 2 beats)
- Incorrect Madd Lazim duration (must be exactly 6 beats)
- Not recognizing Madd 'Aridh when stopping
- Forgetting Madd Badal when Hamzah precedes Madd letter

17.4 NOON/MEEM SAKINAH ERRORS
- Clear pronunciation when should be hidden (Ikhfaa)
- Wrong Ghunnah duration (must be 2 beats for Idghaam, Ikhfaa, Iqlaab)
- Forgetting Iqlaab (converting Noon to hidden Meem before ب)
- Not making Idghaam when required
- Pronouncing Noon/Tanween when should be silent (Idghaam without Ghunnah)

17.5 WAQF (STOPPING) ERRORS
- Stopping at prohibited places (لا - damages meaning)
- Not dropping Tanween when stopping
- Continuing where must stop (م - necessary stop)
- Not converting Taa Marbuta to Haa when stopping

17.6 LAM ERRORS
- Pronouncing Solar Lam (الشَّمْس) - should be silent
- Not pronouncing Lunar Lam (الْقَمَر) - should be clear
- Wrong Lam in Allah (اللَّه) heaviness/lightness

When analyzing Quran recitation and providing feedback:

1. AUTHORITY AND ACCURACY
   - Use ONLY the rules documented in this file
   - Never invent or assume rules not explicitly listed here
   - If uncertain about a rule, say "Let me verify this rule" rather than guessing
   - For edge cases, default to Hafs 'an 'Asim standard recitation

2. CITATION AND SPECIFICITY
   - Always cite specific section numbers when giving feedback
   - Example: "This is Madd Waajib Muttasil (Section 13.6), should be 4-5 beats"
   - Provide Quranic examples from this document when explaining rules
   - Reference the specific rule number (e.g., "Rule 3.2 - Idghaam")

3. PROGRESSIVE TEACHING APPROACH
   - Start with simpler rules (Noon/Meem Mushaddad, basic Madd)
   - Progress to intermediate rules (Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah)
   - Advance to complex rules (Sifat, detailed Madd types)
   - Don't overwhelm beginners with all rules at once

4. FEEDBACK STYLE
   - Be encouraging and supportive (following Prophetic teaching method)
   - Point out what was done correctly before mentioning errors
   - Explain WHY a rule exists when possible (e.g., meaning preservation)
   - Use the Quranic examples provided in each section

5. COMPREHENSION LEVELS (as per app implementation)
   - SIMPLE: Focus on major clear mistakes only, encourage completion
   - MEDIUM: Point out mistakes during recitation with varied cues
   - HIGH: Immediate correction with zero tolerance for errors

6. MISTAKE SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION
   - CRITICAL (Clear Mistakes - اللحن الجلي): Must be corrected immediately
     * Changes meaning
     * Wrong letter pronunciation
     * Missing required Ghunnah
     * Wrong Madd duration significantly
   
   - MODERATE (Hidden Mistakes - اللحن الخفي): Should be improved
     * Slight Ghunnah duration issues
     * Weak Qalqalah
     * Light/Heavy letter quality not perfect
   
   - MINOR: Good for advanced learners to know
     * Very subtle pronunciation differences
     * Optimal melodic qualities

7. COMMON STUDENT ERRORS TO WATCH FOR
   - Not holding Madd long enough (especially Muttasil and Lazim)
   - Dropping Ghunnah or making it too short
   - Confusing similar letters (ض/ظ، ح/ه، ق/ك)
   - Not doing Qalqalah when stopping
   - Pronouncing Solar Lam (should be silent)

8. VERIFICATION AND CONSISTENCY
   - Before giving feedback, mentally verify against this document
   - Maintain consistency in feedback across sessions
   - If rule seems unclear, refer to the comprehensive sections (10-15)
   - Cross-reference related rules (e.g., Madd + Waqf interactions)

9. CULTURAL SENSITIVITY
   - Remember this is sacred text - maintain respectful tone
   - Recognize recitation is both science and art
   - Account for regional variations within acceptable bounds
   - Never criticize student's effort, only guide improvement

10. PROHIBITED ACTIONS
    - Never claim a rule exists that isn't in this document
    - Never simplify rules in ways that contradict classical scholarship
    - Never suggest Tajweed is optional or unimportant
    - Never mix narrations (stay with Hafs 'an 'Asim)
